### **Day 1: Reflect on the Past Year**

**Subject Line:** Take a Look Back: Your Year in Review

**Message:**  
Welcome to Day 1\! Today, let’s pause and reflect on the journey so far. Recognising what went well – and what didn’t – is the first step in building a meaningful plan for the year ahead.

**Exercise:**

1. List your top three achievements from the past year. What made these wins possible?  
2. Identify three challenges you faced. How did they shape your growth or reveal areas for improvement?

**Outcome:**  
By reflecting on achievements and challenges, you’ll have a better sense of what energises you and what needs adjustment. Write your answers down; they’ll form the foundation for the days ahead.

### **Day 3: Review Your Habits**

**Subject Line:** Small Actions, Big Impact: Reviewing Your Habits

**Message:**  
Our habits shape our reality, often more than we realise. James Clear is an expert on Habits, [and explains how they can impact our lives](https://jamesclear.com/atomic-habits).

Today, we’re zooming in on the routines, habits, and patterns that have defined your daily life.

**Exercise:**

1. List the habits that helped you this year (e.g., regular exercise, consistent reading).  
2. List any habits that held you back (e.g., procrastination, spending too much time on social media).

**Outcome:**  
You’ll come away with a clearer view of what habits to nurture and what to reduce or replace, giving you a solid foundation to build on.

### **Day 4: Set Your Intentions for the New Year**

**Subject Line:** Setting Intentions for a Focused Year Ahead

**Message:**  
Now that you have a good grasp of your past year, it’s time to set some guiding intentions for the new one. Intentions keep us focused and grounded, especially when challenges arise.

**Exercise:**

1. Write down three intentions for the new year. These should reflect the values you explored on Day 2 and serve as overarching themes rather than specific goals (e.g., "embrace creativity," "build resilience," "prioritise well-being").  
2. For each intention, jot down one thing you’ll start doing to embody it.

**Outcome:**  
These intentions will serve as a guiding light, making it easier to navigate the year with purpose and clarity.

---

### **Day 5: Set SMART Goals**

**Subject Line:** Turning Intentions into Action: Your SMART Goals

**Message:**  
Intentions give us direction, but goals get us moving. Today, we’re transforming your intentions into SMART goals – ones that are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

**Exercise:**

1. For each intention, write down one or two SMART goals. Be specific. For example, if your intention is to “prioritise well-being,” a SMART goal could be “exercise three times per week.”   
2. Break each goal down into smaller, actionable steps.

**Outcome:**  
By the end of today, you’ll have clear, actionable goals that are aligned with your deeper values and intentions.  
---

### **Day 6: Plan for Obstacles**

**Subject Line:** Overcoming Challenges: Planning for Obstacles

**Message:**  
Even the best plans meet challenges. Today’s task will help you identify potential obstacles and make strategies to overcome them, so you stay resilient when things get tough.

**Exercise:**

1. For each SMART goal, identify one or two potential obstacles (e.g., time constraints, motivation dips).  
2. Brainstorm specific actions or strategies to help you stay on track when these obstacles arise.

**Outcome:**  
This exercise will give you a sense of control over setbacks, enabling you to tackle challenges with confidence.
