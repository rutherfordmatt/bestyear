## Eyebrow

A free exercise · About an hour

## Headline

**Recommended:** Don't make resolutions. *Build your year.*

Alternatives:
- Review the year you had. Build the one you want.
- Most people plan the year ahead. Few review the one behind them.

## Subhead

Build your best year. A free, guided review and plan in seven steps. You'll finish with a one-page vision for the year ahead.

## Three benefit lines

- **Look back honestly.** Your wins, your challenges, and where your energy really went.
- **Anchor it in what matters.** Start from your values, then set goals that survive February.
- **Walk away with a plan.** A beautiful one-page vision to print, pin up and come back to.

## Privacy line

No sign-up needed. Your answers stay in your browser unless you choose to email yourself the plan.

- **Label:** Annual plan

- **2:** Integrity, Family, Creativity, Freedom, Growth, Courage.
- **3:** Putting down the committee, and the phone in bed.
- **4:** Left the job that was draining me, and found my weekends again.
- **5:** Ten paying customers by June. Sleep seven hours a night.

- **7:** On the fridge, checked every three months.
