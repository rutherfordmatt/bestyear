# Life wheel areas

Status: LIVE

Eight areas, shown as sliders from 1 (struggling) to 10 (thriving) and rendered as a radar chart. This is the first thing a visitor does, because it is quick and it produces something to look at straight away.

| Area | One-line description |
| --- | --- |
| Career | Your work, your progress, and whether it still fits |
| Money | How secure and in control you feel, not how much you have |
| Health | Sleep, movement, food and how your body feels |
| Family | Your partner, children, the people closest to you |
| Friends | The wider circle, and feeling part of something |
| Growth | Learning, curiosity and becoming more of who you want to be |
| Fun | Play, rest, adventure and things done purely for joy |
| Purpose | Whether what you spend your days on feels like it matters |

Slider question: How satisfied are you with each area right now?
Hint: Go with your first number. You're measuring how it feels, not how it looks.

Caption under the chart: Your year, in one shape. Lopsided is normal. It's where the next year starts.
