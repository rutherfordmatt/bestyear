# Step 4: Imagine the year ahead

Phase: Turn

Status: DRAFT

## Title

Picture it first

## One-line promise

A clear picture of the year you want, one word to carry it, and three themes to steer by.

## Intro

It's hard to build something you can't picture.

Before any goals, imagine it's twelve months from now and it's been a genuinely good year. Not perfect. Good. What happened? What changed? How do you feel?

From that picture, choose one word to carry with you. It's a compass you can check in two seconds on a bad day. Then choose three themes. Resolutions snap the first time you miss one. Themes bend without breaking.

## Prompts

### Prompt 1: The headline

- **Question:** It's a year from now, and it's been a great year. Write the headline.
- **Placeholder example:** Left the job that was draining me, and found my weekends again.
- **Stuck? hint:** Write it like a friend describing your year to someone else. What would they be most pleased for you about?

### Prompt 2: The detail

- **Question:** What's different about your life? Look at the life wheel if it helps.
- **Placeholder example:** I sleep properly. I see my friends every week. Work feels like mine again.
- **Stuck? hint:** Start with your lowest-scoring areas from Step 1. What would a 7 or 8 look like?

### Prompt 3: Your word

- **Question:** Choose one word for the year ahead.
- **Placeholder example:** Steady
- **Stuck? hint:** Read your headline back. Which word sums it up? The right one usually feels slightly uncomfortable. That's the point.

### Prompt 4: Three themes

- **Question:** What three themes will get you to that headline?
- **Placeholder example:** Protect my energy / Build something of my own / Show up for my people
- **Stuck? hint:** Look at the values you flagged as gaps in Step 2. A theme often lives in that gap.

## Transition line

You can see where you're heading. Next, we turn it into goals you'll know you've hit.

## Interactive output (for reference)

Headline set in large type as a "newspaper from the future" card; word of the year card; three theme cards. The headline and word become the hero of the vision document.
