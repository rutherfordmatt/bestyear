# Step 4: Your compass

Phase: Imagine

Status: LIVE

## Title

Something to *steer by*

## One-line promise

One word to carry through the year, and three themes to hold the course.

## Intro

A picture of the year is no use on a wet Tuesday in February. You need something smaller to carry.

One word as a compass, and three themes. Resolutions snap the first time you miss one; themes bend.

## Prompts

### Prompt 1: Your word

- **Question:** Choose one word for the year ahead.
- **Placeholder example:** Steady
- **Stuck? hint:** Read your headline back. Which word sums it up? The right one usually feels slightly uncomfortable. That's the point.

### Prompt 2: Three themes

- **Question:** What three themes will get you to that headline?
- **Placeholder example:** Protect my energy / Build something of my own / Show up for my people
- **Stuck? hint:** Look at the values you rated lowest in Step 2. A theme often lives in that gap.

## Transition line

You know where you're heading and what you're steering by. Now we turn it into goals you'll know you've hit.

## Interactive output (for reference)

Word of the year card and three theme cards. Your headline from Step 3 is shown above, and the value gaps from Step 2 alongside the themes. Each theme becomes a goal card in Step 5. The word becomes the hero of the vision document alongside the headline.
