# Step 7: Your year, on one page

Phase: Commit

Status: DRAFT

## Title

Your year, *on one page*

## One-line promise

A one-page vision to print, pin up and come back to, with your check-ins already in the diary.

## Intro

Here's everything you've built, in one place. Your headline, your word, your goals and your plan for the bad days, with your values underneath it all.

Read it through once. Change anything that doesn't sound like you.

Then give it a life beyond today. A plan you never look at again was just a nice afternoon. A plan on the fridge door, with a check-in in the diary, is something else entirely.

## Prompts

### Prompt 1: Review

- **Question:** Does this sound like you? Edit anything that doesn't.
- **Stuck? hint:** Read it aloud. Anything that makes you wince is worth rewording.

### Prompt 2: A note to your future self (optional)

- **Question:** Write one line to the person reading this in three months.
- **Placeholder example:** Go gently, and keep going.
- **Stuck? hint:** What will you need to hear when things get hard?

### Prompt 3: Keep it alive

- **Question:** Where will this page live?
- **Options:** On the fridge / By my desk / In my journal / Phone lock screen / Somewhere else
- **Question:** When will you check in on it?
- **Default:** Every three months, with dates suggested from today. Editable.
- **Actions:** Add check-ins to my calendar (.ics download) / Email me a reminder (v1.1)
- **Stuck? hint:** Put the first check-in somewhere you'll actually see it. The first one is the one that matters most.
