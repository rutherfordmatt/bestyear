# Step 5: Goals

Phase: Plan

Status: DRAFT

## Title

Make it real

## One-line promise

Up to three clear goals, one priority, and a habit to carry each of them.

## Intro

Vague goals feel safe because you can never quite fail them. Too many goals feel ambitious and quietly guarantee you'll do none of them.

We keep it tight. Up to three goals, one for each theme. Each gets four answers: what done looks like, why it matters, a date, and the habit that will get you there. Big goals are rarely achieved in big moments. They're achieved in the small thing you do every week.

Then you choose the one that matters most.

## Prompts

(One goal card per theme from Step 4. Maximum three.)

### Prompt 1: What done looks like

- **Question:** How will you know you've done it?
- **Placeholder example:** Ten paying customers for my side project.
- **Stuck? hint:** Imagine a friend asking "did you do it?" in a year's time. What would need to be true for you to say yes without hesitating?

### Prompt 2: Why it matters

- **Question:** Why does this matter to you? Pick the value it serves.
- **Placeholder example:** Creativity. I want to make something that's mine.
- **Stuck? hint:** If it doesn't connect to any of your values, ask whether it's really your goal or one you've borrowed.

### Prompt 3: By when

- **Question:** When will you have done it?
- **Placeholder example:** End of June
- **Stuck? hint:** Pick a date that makes you slightly nervous but not panicked.

### Prompt 4: The habit that carries it

- **Question:** What's the small, regular thing that will get you there?
- **Placeholder example:** Two hours on it every Saturday morning, before anything else.
- **Stuck? hint:** Make it so small it feels almost silly. Small habits survive busy weeks. Big ones don't.

### Prompt 5: First step

- **Question:** What will you do this week to get started?
- **Placeholder example:** Talk to three people who might buy it.
- **Stuck? hint:** If it would take more than an hour, it's not the first step. Break it down again.

### Prompt 6: The priority

- **Question:** If you could only achieve one of these, which would it be?
- **Stuck? hint:** Which one would make the others easier, or matter less?

## Transition line

You've got the goals. Now let's plan for the moments they'll be tested, and the people who'll help.

## Interactive output (for reference)

Up to three goal cards, each tagged with its theme and value, showing its habit and first step. The priority goal is starred and leads the vision document.
