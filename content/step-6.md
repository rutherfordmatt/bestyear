# Step 6: Obstacles and support

Phase: Plan

Status: LIVE

## Title

Plan for the *bad days*

## One-line promise

A ready-made response for the moments most likely to knock you off course, and the people who'll help you back on.

## Intro

Optimism is lovely. It's also a terrible plan.

Things will get in the way. Work will spike, motivation will dip, life will happen. The people who keep going aren't more disciplined. They decided in advance what they'd do when it happened, and they didn't try to do it alone.

For each goal, name the most likely obstacle and write your response. The research behind this kind of if-then planning is strong. Then decide who's in your corner.

## Prompts

### Prompt 1: If

(Shown once per goal from Step 5.)

- **Question:** What's most likely to get in the way? Finish the sentence: If...
- **Placeholder example:** ...work runs late on a Friday and I'm too tired on Saturday,
- **Stuck? hint:** Be specific about the trigger. "If I'm busy" is too vague. Look inward too. Often the biggest obstacle is a thought, like "I'm not ready yet".

### Prompt 2: Then

- **Question:** What will you do instead? Finish the sentence: then I will...
- **Placeholder example:** ...do one hour on Sunday instead, no matter what.
- **Stuck? hint:** Aim for the minimum that keeps you moving, not a heroic recovery.

### Prompt 3: Who's in your corner

- **Question:** Who will you share this plan with?
- **Options (pick any):** A partner / A friend / A colleague or mentor / A coach / A group or community / Just me for now
- **Follow-up:** What will you ask them for?
- **Placeholder example:** Ask Alex to check in on my Saturday mornings once a month.
- **Stuck? hint:** People who share their goals with someone they respect are more likely to follow through. Pick one person and one specific ask.
